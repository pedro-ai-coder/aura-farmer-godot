# Brand direction — keep your shape

**Candidate system.** [GDD §8](../../GDD.md) and exact existing art approvals remain authoritative.

## Position and voice

CLUTCHPUNKS makes the pressure of online attention tangible through funny, expressive cartoon action. The player fantasy is keeping control of who you are while the crowd tries to define you. The game should feel inviting to the player and defiant toward the system; it should not insult beginners or demand fluency in disposable slang.

**Recommended campaign line:** KEEP YOUR SHAPE.

Alternatives: **MAKE YOUR OWN SIGNAL.** (world-facing, less distinctive); **STILL HERE. STILL YOU.** (character-facing, less action-oriented). These are proposals, not cleared trademarks. “Built Different. Play Different.” remains historical candidate copy; it is not the recommendation because it could describe almost any action game.

Write from a character's immediate situation. “That comment hit a wall” shows the premise; “Experience a disruptive ecosystem” explains nothing. Keep jokes short enough to survive localization. Reserve mystery for story material; availability and controls must be literal.

## Color roles

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep ink | `#13233A` | Main field and body text on light surfaces |
| Warm paper | `#F7EDCD` | Wordmark and reading surfaces |
| Coral | `#E57461` | One editorial intervention; paired with text/shape |
| Sky | `#4EA3FF` | Secondary navigation/focus experiments |
| Gold | `#F8C45F` | Rare campaign emphasis; not a new resource |

Aim for approximately 80% ink/paper, 15% existing hero color, 5% accent in marketing layouts. This is a composition target, not a runtime recoloring instruction. Keep body text ink-on-paper or paper-on-ink. Measure contrast in the implementation; do not use coral-on-paper for small body text. Existing Aura, healing, rarity and danger semantics remain unchanged.

## Lettering and typography

The selected logo gets bespoke editable lettering; no stock typeface is presented as the proprietary mark. For contextual text, reuse the repository's licensed Barlow baseline. Archivo/Hero Clash may appear only as a labeled candidate where its manifest has candidate status. Do not redistribute a newly downloaded font or infer approval from a mockup.

For website proposals: body 18 px with 1.5 line height, metadata 12–14 px, headings 40–72 px desktop / 32–44 px mobile. Sentence case for reading; uppercase for short labels only. These are web design starting values, not Godot UI changes.

## Composition and graphic vocabulary

Place one hero or one story object in a dominant region. Use an offset edge or open counter to connect title and illustration. Reading blocks use flat rules, not repeated rounded dashboard cards. Maintain a clear text area; never put a busy letter counter over a face, weapon or CTA.

The permanent logo and each season are separate layers. Season 01 can use paired contradictory captions and interrupted records; future seasons can change the editorial material without mutating the brand silhouette. A chapter identifier is metadata, never a new logo.

Approved character cutouts retain their exact colors and silhouette. Show three paired kit groups, not six independent powers. Give each group equal presence across the page; the example application may spotlight a single appearance without implying it is the only hero.

## Motion grammar

Propose a 180–240 ms compression/release of the surrounding frame, followed by stillness. Keep the wordmark readable throughout. An incident cut happens once on deliberate entry, not every scroll event. Reduced motion immediately shows the final composition with identical copy. No flicker, autoplay sound, perpetual jitter, fake live counters or combat overlays are commissioned here.

## Concrete do / don't

| Do | Reject |
| --- | --- |
| Let one C/P counter define an unmistakable opening | Slice every letter and add random RGB offsets |
| Put a precise contradiction next to an in-world record | Clone a social network's profile/feed layout |
| Show Pixel Vixen using her approved portrait | Generate a new costume to match a marketing palette |
| Keep season line subordinate to the full name | Make “The Feed Is Broken” look like the game title |
| Use honest “Read the Incident” navigation | Put “Play Now” on an inaccessible closed test |
| Give hero humor a clear situation | Stack slang, skulls, crowns and esports badges |

## Source acceptance

The dated GDD acceptance of the female V2 assets supersedes their historical manifest's pending labels for those exact hashes. Keep both provenance records; this package does not rewrite that history. New logo and application images remain unapproved, even when they sit next to approved sources.
