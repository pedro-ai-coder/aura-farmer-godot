# Season 01 — THE FEED IS BROKEN

**Internal CEO story presentation. Contains proposed finale spoilers.** Adapted from the [world bible](../../narrative/CLUTCHPUNKS_WORLD_BIBLE_V0_1.md) and [Season 01 bible](../../narrative/SEASON_01_THE_FEED_IS_BROKEN_V0_1.md), PR #100. Detailed fiction remains pending editorial acceptance.

## 1 / A comment leaves the screen

Ruin Alley knows how to live with noise. Then an argument leaves a mark on a wall. Two recordings show different versions of the same street. The Feed calls it a correction.

The proposed world rule is simple: amplified attention can distort physical reality and its record. This is fiction, not a conversion of likes into XP, Aura or combat damage. Aura remains the canonical Flex resource. Nothing in the story deletes a player's save.

## 2 / Someone refuses the assigned role

The Clutchpunks are people who refuse to let the loudest reaction decide who they are. One playable protagonist carries the run; the presentation must work for any canonical kit and either appearance.

| Kit / appearances | Beginning | Season growth, proposed |

These are three narrative lenses, not a six-person cast, new classes or branching gameplay requirements.

## 3 / The first face of the problem

Big Hater cannot tolerate someone else becoming the main character. He benefits from the outrage and mistakes that advantage for control. Defeating him is a real local win. It does not explain who operates The Feed.

The boss, Ruin Alley and current encounters are approved gameplay. His motive and dialogue are narrative proposals. Five 120-second waves, four event bosses, the 10:00 final boss and canonical Berserk remain intact.

## 5 / The victory that opens the next question

**Chapter 01 aftermath candidate, only after a real victory:**

> LOCAL HOST DISCONNECTED.
> UPSTREAM SIGNAL: ACTIVE.

Let the local street fall quiet before the second line appears. A loss does not play this sting, grant a win or unlock fictional account progression.

**Proposed finale:** The city keeps evidence of what happened. The final transmission states, “THE FEED IS NOT BROKEN. IT IS WORKING EXACTLY AS INTENDED.” The trace resolves to **ORIGIN / CROWN COURT**.

The season closes its local conflict while opening the larger one. Crown Court is a future narrative destination, not a currently selectable level, a confirmed Season 02 release or proof of the system's ultimate creator.
