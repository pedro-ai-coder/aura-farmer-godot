## 2. Audience, meaning, pronunciation

**Positioning:** A personality-forward cartoon action roguelite about rebellious outsiders breaking a manufactured social-media reality.
**Audience target hypothesis:** Gaming-native players who appreciate expressive characters, high-signal combat, streamer-friendly jokes and internet culture, without restricting the game to a single age cohort.
**Name reading:** CLUTCH (win under pressure) + PUNKS (rebellious crew). The compound should be one visual unit, not two distant brand names.

**One-sentence elevator pitch:**
"CLUTCHPUNKS is a fast, character-driven action survivor where internet chaos becomes real and outcast heroes fight to break the Feed."

**Emotional markers:** defiant, kinetic, clever, welcoming, weird, independent.
**Avoid:** generic e-sports aggression, faux-military UI, cryptocurrency aesthetics, mass-produced cyberpunk neon, dating/social platform cues, ironic hostility directed at players.

## 3. Logo development — three original candidate routes

These are **art briefs**, not approved raster/vector assets. All use original hand-built shapes and lettering, with no unauthorized third-party font or logo copying.

### Route A — BROKEN BROADCAST (recommended first exploration)

- A distinctive compact custom **CLUTCHPUNKS** wordmark, angular but readable, with a deliberately interrupted baseline or small split in a letter connection.
- The wordmark feels like an outlawed broadcast poster, not a glitch-effect preset.
- One recognizable emblem: a short broken-signal bolt/chevron that can survive at 32×32 px. Do not use a generic Wi-Fi symbol as the whole identity.
- Treat "CLUTCH" and "PUNKS" as one coherent silhouette; small optical distinction permitted with a single gold or coral accent.
- Strong ink outline, cream fills, blue structural shadow, restrained disruptive coral. No photographic textures.
- Works over dark navy, cream, grayscale and a small mobile icon.

### Route B — CREW MARK

- The wordmark incorporates a custom insignia usable as an in-world graffiti tag.
- Emphasizes belonging, friendship and rebellion; avoid looking like a sports team logo.
- Works on website hero profiles, Archive cards and optional later cosmetics (none approved now).

### Route C — SIGNAL CUT

- Lean geometric logo with clean slashed letter joints and a small static interference motif.
- Best for enigmatic narrative marketing; risks being too clinical for the colorful game.
- Requires testing against actual approved hero key art before promotion.

**Initial recommendation:** Concept A as an original candidate alongside a monochrome test. Do **not** replace the current approved wordmark/logo bytes with this text specification alone.

## 7. Required art package before runtime or launch acceptance

- Editable original logo master(s), with documented authorship/provenance and redistribution rights.
- Exact PNG/SVG export(s) at relevant sizes; transparent and dark/light versions, icon-safe crop.
- Pixel-grid and responsive tests at 32 px, 64 px, 320 px and desktop title scale.
- Basic black/white legibility and monochrome fallback.
- Wordmark/color/clear-space guides and localized subtitle placement.
- Reference mockups: website landing at 1440×900 and 390×844; game title at 1600×900 and mobile landscape; character selection and season chapter header.
- Explicit creator approval referencing **exact source files and hashes** before replacing any approved wordmark or promoting new identity assets.
- Check naming/trademark/domain/store/search collision in target jurisdictions **before public brand migration**. Initial ideation research is not legal clearance.
- Never rely on a rendered concept image as production source without reconstructing clean editable art and verifying licensing.

## 8. Acceptance criteria for brand direction

1. At a glance, users can read **CLUTCHPUNKS**, not "CLUTCH" or "PUNKS" as unrelated products.
2. It reads as an original cartoon action game, not a social network app or generic e-sports team.
3. Season 01 treatment is subordinate to the timeless core name.
4. Works with approved six appearances and existing Clean Cut UI without changing canonical game content.
5. Still feels appropriate for Crown Court and Toxic Garden narrative directions.
6. Wordmark is accessible, responsive and robust without animation, glow, textures or a background image.
7. Creator approves exact artwork after reviewing actual context captures.
