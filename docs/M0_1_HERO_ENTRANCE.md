# M0.1 hero arena entrance V1

**CANDIDATE** choreography, light, sounds, pose sequencing and dialogue.

| Beat | Time | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | 0–2.45 s | Close rear-view walk. The foreground frame slides away independently of the floor. Existing covers depth-sort around the hero. |
| Arena reveal | 2.45–4.45 s | The camera widens and pans toward the arena. Ruin Alley uses right-side cyan light and magenta trim. MSG Factory enters from the opposite side with amber light, lime trim and existing machinery. |
| Signature | 4.45–6.05 s | Close front framing uses a staged pose from the selected hero's atlas, with a per-identity hold and candidate catchphrase. |
| Handoff | 6.05–10.25 s | The shot aligns to the gameplay camera and legal spawn at 7.25 s. After 3–2–1, Solo initializes the normal run and Wave Music V1. |

The production map picker invokes the director. During the entrance the gameplay clock, RNG, spawning and score are idle. Skip and Escape share the guarded callback to `start_run`. Retry calls `start_run` directly. Reduced Motion uses fixed compositions and a static countdown. Cue sounds honor the existing Master bus and stop on Skip. Existing opt-in gameplay QA fixtures bypass the entrance.

## Asset provenance

The new framing and light are CanvasItem geometry. Three candidate cues are deterministic PCM generated by the existing `make_clip` synthesizer with no external samples. Candidate dialogue is unvoiced. No new source imagery or audio files were imported.

## Validation and rollback

- `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/test_hero_entrance.gd` compares normal completion and Skip against direct Solo initialization across the roster. Use an isolated test profile.
- `godot --path . --fixed-fps 30 --write-movie <output.avi> --script res://tests/capture_hero_entrance.gd` records the ordinary Garage and map confirmation through the first enemy spawn. Add `-- --factory --vixen` for the second pairing. Use `-- --mobile --stills <existing-folder>` for compact native frames, with optional `--portrait --reduced-motion`.
- The close crop exposes the resolution of the existing plates. The signatures are based on existing anticipation drawings. Bespoke acting could extend this later without changing the handoff.
- Roll back with a revert of this implementation commit. It adds no save schema or migration.
