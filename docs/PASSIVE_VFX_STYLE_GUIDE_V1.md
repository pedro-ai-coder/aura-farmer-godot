# CLUTCHPUNKS Combat VFX Style Guide V1

**PROPOSED** · Creator review begins with Six → Seven.

- **Show the source, range and outcome.** A compact hero-foot cue marks 6/7. The burst originates at the seventh contact. The broken perimeter marks the actual 90 U edge. Short paired marks at enemy feet represent confirmed HP loss.
- **Keep combat silhouettes clear.** Draw broad area treatments on the ground. Use narrow marks near feet so the player can see enemy poses, HP and intent. Give boss telegraphs and hostile projectiles the last draw pass. Keep shield and hurt feedback readable during major damage events.
- **Use compact silhouettes and distinct timing.** A sharp outward kick, swept facets and a rapid dissolve make the blast read as a single event. Dark edging is visible against bright pavement. The warm accent and violet core are color studies without canonical identity assignments. Under Reduced Motion keep the perimeter, contact marks and contrast while removing expansion, rotation and bright radial flashes.
- **Distinguish defense from recovery.** A protective outline indicates a real positive grant. An absorption tick represents a resolved block. Active shield styling tracks remaining protection and expiry. Healing emphasis uses restored HP and stays close to the recipient.
- **Let temporary buffs describe their scope.** Use a low-energy boundary for a live zone, one transition cue for entry or expiry, and separate significant stack thresholds from hits. Keep persistent numerical modifiers in acquisition and inspection.
- **Budget overlapping effects.** Prefer one major footprint per source, compact per-target contacts, and brief fades. Spend emphasis on the owner's threatened state or a large confirmed result. Preserve announced boundaries when cosmetic detail is reduced. Maintain meaningful contrast and shape through downscaling.

Review the normal, quarter-speed, dense overlap and Reduced Motion recordings at their intended size. Check whether a viewer can locate the center and unaffected neighboring enemy with the game HUD hidden. Review ordinary hurt and hostile telegraphs during the burst before approving extension to the next P0 passive.
