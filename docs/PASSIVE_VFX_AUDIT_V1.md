## Existing gaps to resolve before production integration

- **Gameplay semantics.** The current call sites qualify once per shared area activation or per projectile's first primary contact. They can advance on shield-only contacts, and the second enemy of a piercing projectile does not advance the counter again. GDD's “primary hit” needs explicit reconciliation with these existing rules before any change. The candidate uses the runtime's trigger and reads only actual HP loss for secondary marks. The same shared path feeds Homie Check and Let Him Cook.
- **Gameplay semantics.** No U's debuff loop can set weaken on an enemy with a future `born` time. The damage resolver independently rejects that enemy. A later No U renderer must receive separate health-loss and weaken records and must retain the behavior until an authorized rules change.
- **Presentation gaps.** Big Chad and emergency shields share the shield meter and common absorption notice. Homie Check can reset its counter at full HP without effective healing. So Back's tick count reports scheduled ticks, including capped ticks. These rules call for separate result cues rather than inferred hits or recovery.
- **Presentation gap.** The legacy area rings use the same visual motif for Six → Seven and No U. Their animated rims start at 85% of the true radius. Six → Seven's regular hit sparks do not carry source identity and the baseline HUD can miss a killing burst when victory changes mode.
- **Scope limits.** No multiplayer implementation or device measurements were used to classify this Solo audit. Team recipients, exact Phaser parity beyond the existing scoped rules, and final dense creator judgment remain separate acceptance work.

Future-only `get-ratiod`, `ego-check`, `uno-reverse`, `brainrot-rain`, and `take-l` are excluded from the lab and the playable proposal. The icon registry carries their approved illustrative resources.
